The Tonique Experience

Tonique kalyani nagar is Maharashtras second super-premium liquor outlet after its flagship store in Kalyani Nagar, Pune. It offers a curated range of premium and luxury liquor brands at MRP, ensuring authenticity and quality.

The store also hosts exclusive, invitation-only tasting sessions for a refined experience. With a wide selection of liquor accessories, Tonique Kalyani Nagar serves as a complete destination for all your party and lifestyle needs.
